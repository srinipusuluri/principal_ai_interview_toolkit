# Principal AI Engineer & Architect Interview Toolkit

A single-page, practice-first study guide: 8 domains, 80 interview questions with model answers and common pitfalls, a whiteboard drill per domain, and 66 link-checked learning resources.

Open `index.html` in a browser (no build step). Progress and stars are saved locally in your browser.

Domains: Python & AI engineering, LLMs, RAG, agentic AI & MCP, enterprise architecture, cloud & infrastructure, AI security & governance, evaluation & observability.
