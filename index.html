🧭 Playbook: how to use this

Answers are hidden by default. Say your answer aloud for 90 seconds, then reveal, compare, and tick “Reviewed”. Star anything you stumbled on and revisit it with “Starred only”.

1FrameState the goal, users, constraints and what “good” looks like (SLO, quality bar, cost).
2DesignGive a simple baseline first, then the components and data flow.
3Trade offCompare two options out loud. Name what you gave up and why.
4HardenFailure modes, security, abuse cases, rollback and cost limits.
5MeasureClose with metrics, an eval plan, and a real outcome from your experience.

Suggested 4-week plan

Week 1: FoundationsDomains 1–2. Build a small FastAPI + LLM service with retries, streaming, structured output and tests.
Week 2: Knowledge & agentsDomains 3–4. Build hybrid RAG with an eval set, then a tool-using agent with budgets.
Week 3: Architecture & platformDomains 5–6. Do two written ADRs and one full system-design drill on a whiteboard.
Week 4: Trust & proofDomains 7–8. Threat-model your own project, then run mock interviews with the Random button.

Questions worth asking the interviewer

  • How do you decide today that an AI feature is good enough to ship, and who owns that call?
  • What share of incidents last year came from model behaviour versus integration, data or permissions?
  • Where does the platform team end and product teams begin? What is the paved road?
  • What would success look like for this role at 6 and 12 months?
